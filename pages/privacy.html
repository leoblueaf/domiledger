Privacy Policy

Last updated {UPDATED}

DomiLedger is designed to keep your home information private and under your control.

Information stored on your device

Your DomiLedger home records are stored locally on your device. This includes information such as:

  • Home details and selected home features
  • Maintenance tasks and completion history
  • Appliance and system information
  • Model and serial numbers
  • Warranty dates
  • Costs and notes
  • Photos and attachments added to your records

DomiLedger does not require an account. DomiLedger does not operate a cloud database containing your home records. DomiLedger does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party behavioral advertising.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a photo to an appliance record, iOS asks for permission to use the camera or your photo library. Only the photos you pick are copied into DomiLedger's private storage on your device. You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

DomiLedger uses Apple StoreKit to offer and manage in-app purchases. Purchase transactions are processed by Apple. DomiLedger does not receive or store your payment-card information. The app may use StoreKit on your device to determine whether DomiLedger Pro features are currently available to you.

Product recall lookups

DomiLedger can check public product-recall information provided by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC). When a recall lookup is performed, the request contains the appliance brand name so relevant public recall notices can be retrieved.

Your appliance model number, serial number, home information, photos, maintenance history, notes, and costs are not sent as part of the recall lookup. Model matching is performed on your device.

Recall results may be incomplete, delayed, or may include products that are not yours. Always verify recall information with the CPSC or the product manufacturer before taking action. DomiLedger is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission.

Backups and exports

DomiLedger allows you to export a backup file containing your home records, and to create a Home Ledger PDF. You choose when and where to save or share those files. DomiLedger does not upload them to a DomiLedger server. Once exported, a file is under your control, and its privacy depends on where you choose to store or share it.

Notifications

Maintenance reminders and warranty alerts are scheduled locally on your device. DomiLedger does not operate a notification server and does not send your home information to a server to generate reminders.

External links

DomiLedger may contain links that you choose to open, such as links to official recall information or support resources. When you open an external website, that site may receive normal web-request information and will operate under its own privacy practices.

Support email

If you contact DomiLedger support, we will receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request and is not used for advertising or behavioral tracking. Please avoid including sensitive personal or home information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Deleting your data

Your DomiLedger records remain on your device unless you delete them or remove the app. Deleting DomiLedger from your device removes the app's locally stored data from that device. Backup files and PDFs you previously exported are separate files and must be deleted from wherever you chose to save them.

Children

DomiLedger is a general home-management utility and is not designed or directed specifically to children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if DomiLedger's features or data practices change. The current version will always be available on this page.
