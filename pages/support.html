How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open DomiLedger, go to the Ledger tab, and select Restore purchases. DomiLedger will ask Apple to restore eligible purchases associated with your Apple Account. You should not be charged again for an existing lifetime purchase or an active subscription.

How do I back up my home?

Open the Ledger tab and choose Back up data. DomiLedger creates a backup file and you choose where to save it. The file is not uploaded to a DomiLedger server. Keep it somewhere you control, such as iCloud Drive or Files, so you can reach it if you replace or lose your phone.

How do I restore a backup?

Open the Ledger tab and select Restore from backup. Choose a DomiLedger backup file and confirm. Restoring replaces what is currently in the app with the contents of the backup. Restoring a backup is free and does not require DomiLedger Pro.

How do I move DomiLedger to a new iPhone?

Before changing phones, export a current backup from your old device. Install DomiLedger on the new device, restore your purchase if needed, then use Restore from backup to import your home records. Keep the original backup until you've confirmed everything appears correctly on the new device.

How accurate are the product-recall results?

DomiLedger searches public CPSC recall information and performs appliance-model matching on your device. A model match means the model you saved appears to correspond with a recall notice. It does not independently confirm that your exact appliance is affected. Brand-and-category matches are intentionally shown more cautiously.

Always verify the model, manufacturing dates, serial range, and remedy with the CPSC or the manufacturer before acting. DomiLedger is not affiliated with or endorsed by the CPSC.

How do I cancel my annual subscription?

DomiLedger Pro Annual is managed through Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select DomiLedger, and follow Apple's cancellation options. Your Pro access normally continues through the end of the period you already paid for. The Lifetime purchase does not renew.

How do I delete my DomiLedger data?

Your home records are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes DomiLedger's stored data from that device. Any backup files or Home Ledger PDFs you previously saved are separate files; delete those from Files, iCloud Drive, or wherever you stored them.

What is the Home Ledger PDF?

The Home Ledger PDF is a readable history of your home details, appliances and maintenance records in DomiLedger. It's useful for your own records or when sharing home history with a buyer, inspector, or contractor. It reflects what was recorded in DomiLedger; it is not an inspection, appraisal, warranty, certification, or independently verified service record.

Does DomiLedger upload my home information?

No. There is no DomiLedger account or cloud database. Your home records, appliance details, costs, photos, and maintenance history stay on your device. Recall lookups send an appliance brand name to the public CPSC recall service; model matching happens on your phone. Apple processes App Store purchases separately. See the privacy policy.

Why didn't I receive a reminder?

Check that notifications are enabled for DomiLedger in iPhone Settings > Notifications, and check the task's next-due date in the app. iOS controls notification delivery, and settings such as Focus modes can affect when notifications appear.
